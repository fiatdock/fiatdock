# I measured every paid x402 call in a 30-day window. Here is where the money actually goes.

> Read on 21 September 2026 (09:49 UTC) from Coinbase's public x402 discovery index. Canonical, with tables: https://fiatdock.com/x402-demand.html

Coinbase publishes a discovery index of x402 resources — every endpoint that opted into the
Bazaar extension and has settled a payment through the CDP facilitator — and for each one it
reports the calls and unique paying wallets of the last 30 days. It is public and keyless. I read
all of it: **15,133 resources on 2,000 hosts**, and **857,072 paid calls** in the window.

Five things fell out that I have not seen stated anywhere, and one of them changes what "a payer"
means.

## 1. Half of all the money goes to two endpoints

| | share of all paid calls |
|---|---|
| the single largest resource | **39.0%** |
| top 10 resources | **75.9%** |
| top 50 resources | 85.5% |
| **top 1% of resources** (≈150) | **90.5%** |

Half of every call in the index lands on the **top two** resources. The top hosts:

| host | calls / 30d | share |
|---|---|---|
| blockrun.ai | 347,102 | 40.5% |
| ax1.vc | 168,128 | 19.6% |
| stableenrich.dev | 59,909 | 7.0% |
| agents.chain.link | 56,073 | 6.5% |
| win.oneshotagent.com | 29,898 | 3.5% |
| x402.sniperx.fun | 27,387 | 3.2% |
| api.onesource.io | 16,047 | 1.9% |

Two thousand hosts are listed. Seven of them carry 82% of the volume.

## 2. "Unique payers" mostly counts crawlers paying once

The index reports `l30DaysUniquePayers` per resource, and it is tempting to read it as customers.
Check it against calls:

- **8,875 resources (58.7% of everything with a paid call) have calls exactly equal to payers** —
  every wallet that ever paid them paid exactly once. Together they carry **1.8%** of all calls.
- 9,442 resources (62.5%) sit below 1.5 calls per payer.
- **10,552 resources have exactly one payer.** Only 66 have fifty or more.

That is the signature of catalog sweeps: wallets that pay every endpoint once to index it, score
it, or research it. A payer count is a measure of how many crawlers have found you. It is not
demand.

## 3. The market is 4.6% of the index

Define *repeat purchase* as a resource with at least 20 calls in 30 days **and** at least 3 calls
per paying wallet — someone came back. That filter keeps **699 resources on 234 hosts**: 4.6% of
what has a paid call.

Those 699 resources carry **93.0% of all calls**.

So the honest description of x402 today is not "15,000 services". It is roughly seven hundred
services that people return to, plus fourteen thousand that have been indexed.

## 4. What repeat buyers actually pay for

I classified the top 40 repeat-purchase resources by hand (84% of all volume) after finding the
keyword buckets too rough. Rounded:

| what it is | calls / 30d in the top 40 | examples |
|---|---|---|
| **LLM inference and agent endpoints** | ~347,000 | chat completions at $0.002/request (334k calls, 183 payers, ~1,800 calls each); Anthropic-compatible messages; grounded answers |
| **AI verdicts on onchain data** | ~168,000 | "send a Base token contract, get an AI-written verdict" — $0.02, **2,510 payers**, the widest customer base in the index |
| **web search and page reading** | ~83,000 | Exa neural search (46k calls, 194 payers), Firecrawl, page→markdown, SERP, X/Twitter search |
| **execution and workflow** | ~61,000 | Chainlink workflow execution; send-token execution fees |
| **trading signals and token analytics** | ~36,000 | smart-money / KOL / whale flows per token; Solana token authenticity; liquidation maps |
| **enrichment and identity** | ~23,000 | email deliverability and validation, LinkedIn/company/person enrichment, people search |

**Not one of the top 40 is a raw chain read** — no gas price, block number, balance lookup or
token price feed. The keyword pass over the whole index says the same thing more roughly:
commodity chain reads are **2.6%** of all calls and **1.2%** of repeat-purchase calls; plain
market prices **2.2%**; CAPTCHA solving **0.3%**.

The pattern is not "crypto sells" or "data sells". It is: **things a public RPC or a free API
answers for nothing do not earn; things that are derived, computed or searched do.** Inference is
the largest category by a wide margin, and one inference endpoint alone is 39% of the index.

## 5. The money is at fractions of a cent

| | p25 | median | p75 | call-weighted median |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| all paid resources | $0.003 | $0.010 | $0.020 | **$0.005** |
| repeat-purchase resources | $0.003 | $0.010 | $0.025 | **$0.003** |

67.8% of resources are priced at one cent or less, and they carry 68.9% of calls. At the other
end, **298 resources are priced at $1 or more; between them they took 1,791 calls in 30 days.**
Repeat buyers pay a third of a cent per call and call a lot.

And it is a Base market: **93.9% of calls settle on `eip155:8453`**, 5.2% on Solana, everything
else rounds to zero.

## Three traps in reading this index

1. **A single payer can be a single integration.** `agents.chain.link` shows 38,841 calls from
   one wallet. That is one relationship, not a market, and any "calls per payer" ranking puts it at
   the top. Weight by payers before imitating anyone.
2. **The 30-day figures move by whole resources.** My read on 2026-09-20 showed 479,100 calls; on
   2026-09-21, 857,072. The difference is essentially one endpoint entering the window with 334k
   calls. Date any number you quote from this index, and expect the headline to swing.
3. **Categories are a judgement.** The keyword buckets over 15,000 descriptions are approximate
   (26.5% of calls fall into "other"), which is why §4 is hand-classified and limited to the top 40.
   "AI verdict on a token" sits between inference and chain analytics; I put it in its own row.

Also: the index covers resources that opted into discovery **and** settled through the CDP
facilitator. Payments through other facilitators, and endpoints that never declared themselves,
are not in it. Everything here is Coinbase's own accounting; I did not audit the settlements.

## If you sell over x402

- Sell something derived: a verdict, a search, an enrichment, a completion. A field a public node
  already serves will be indexed and paid for once by every crawler, and then not again.
- Price in the $0.002–$0.01 range and expect volume, not margin. The $1+ tier is empty of calls.
- Be on Base. The rest is a rounding error today.
- Expect one integration to dominate your numbers, and measure your payer count against your
  call count before believing either.

## Reproduce it

Read `https://api.cdp.coinbase.com/platform/v2/x402/discovery/resources?limit=100&offset=N`
until `pagination.total` is exhausted. For each item take `quality.l30DaysTotalCalls`,
`quality.l30DaysUniquePayers`, `accepts[0].amount` (atomic USDC, divide by 1e6) and
`accepts[0].network`. Repeat purchase = calls ≥ 20 and calls / payers ≥ 3. Everything above is a
sort, a sum and a quantile over those four fields; the script is about 120 lines.
